# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience: Inbound clients, technical founders, startup leads, and teams seeking a versatile developer who can build production frontend applications while independently handling UI design, content/copy, and automated workflows.

User situation & job to be done:
- Evaluating whether Prosper has authentic, production-grade craft and technical reliability.
- Reviewing tangible evidence of past work across 4 distinct disciplines (frontend, graphics, copywriting, automation).
- Testing running websites directly via live embeds or high-fidelity visual proofs.
- Establishing direct contact to commission a build, contract, or ongoing maintenance.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio and interactive showcase for Prosper Kayode. It demonstrates multi-disciplinary execution across four practices without diluting technical authority. Success means converting visitors into inbound client inquiries with high confidence in both engineering and aesthetic standards.

## Positioning

"Most clients arrive with one problem and leave having solved three."
Unlike conventional single-discipline developers who stop at markup, Prosper bridges the gap between engineering, brand design, editorial typesetting, and AI automation. He takes raw Figma designs to live production, formats manuscripts into published books, designs brand systems, and automates multi-step business workflows.

## Operating Context

- Browsed on desktop and mobile viewports by clients, founders, and engineers evaluating capability.
- Visitors often browse with tight attention spans; work must lead immediately with proof, interactive demonstrations, and clear scope notes.
- Frontend projects feature live in-browser preview frames allowing real site interaction directly within the card.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Four core practice filters: Frontend Development, Graphics Design, Content & Copywriting, and AI Automation.
- Live interactive website previews embedded in sandboxed frames for verified production URLs (`cakesnpastries.com.ng`, `smartcbt.com.ng`, `facons.edu.ng`, `topvision.ng`), with fallback to static screenshots.
- Lightbox asset viewer with keyboard navigation (`ArrowLeft`, `ArrowRight`, `Escape`).
- Zero reliance on external icon packs or heavy animation runtimes; hardware-composited transitions only.
- Strict performance budget: fast initial paint, optimized responsive typography (`clamp`), zero scroll-trapping on mobile.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Prosper Kayode.
- Aesthetic tone: Editorial, restrained, authentic, craftsman-like. Monospaced metadata paired with serif editorial text and clear sans display typography.
- Signature identity: Calligraphic loading screen with Runethia font ("Prosper Kayode") smoothly introducing the portfolio.
- Palette & theme: Warm natural paper background (`#EEEFEA` light / `#111412` dark) with deep forest green accent (`#1F5245` light / `#7FBFA8` dark) and crisp hairline borders (`#D8DAD2` / `#2A302C`).

## Product Principles

1. **Proof Over Claims**: Every practice is backed by concrete visual proof, live running previews, execution metrics, or explicit architectural pipeline breakdowns.
2. **Restrained Editorial Tone**: Understated, honest copy with zero hyperbole or inflated claims; clear attribution of exact personal scope.
3. **Seamless Responsiveness**: Mobile is a first-class citizen; all interactive frames, thumbnails, and navigation fit naturally on small viewports without horizontal clipping or scroll traps.
4. **Performance by Default**: Lightweight assets, native compositor animations, zero heavy dependencies.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliance for color contrast across light and dark modes.
- Full keyboard navigability (skip-to-content link, focus rings, accessible modal dialogs).
- Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` across all transitions and preloader animations.
- Semantic HTML markup (`article`, `section`, `nav`, `dl`, `dt`, `dd`, `blockquote`).
